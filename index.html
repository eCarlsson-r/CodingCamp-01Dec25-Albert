About the Company

RevoU is one of Indonesia's leading online education technology (EdTech) platforms, focusing on equipping students with high-demand digital skills. It operates as an immersive "bootcamp" academy designed to bridge the gap between traditional education and the needs of the modern tech industry.

Here is a brief breakdown of what they offer:

RevoU specializes in intensive courses that typically run for 3–6 months. Their flagship programs include:

  • Full-Stack Digital Marketing
  • Full-Stack Data Analytics
  • Software Engineering
  • Product Management
  • AI Academy (specialized shorter courses on Artificial Intelligence)

Unlike self-paced video courses (like Coursera or Udemy), RevoU emphasizes live, interactive learning.

  • Live Classes: Classes are held online in real-time, usually outside of working hours (evenings/weekends) to accommodate working professionals.
  • Mentorship: Students are assigned mentors (industry practitioners) who grade assignments and provide personal feedback.
  • Project-Based: The curriculum is heavy on practical application, often requiring students to work on real-world projects or simulations.

RevoU is best known for its aggressive career support features:

  • RevoU Next: A specialized career coaching module that helps graduates with CV creation, portfolio building, and mock interviews.
  • Job Guarantee: For eligible students, they offer a tuition refund (up to 100%) if the student does not secure a job in the industry within 6 months of graduation (terms and conditions apply).
  • BNSP Certification: Many of their programs now offer certification aligned with the Indonesian National Professional Certification Agency (BNSP), adding formal recognition to their certificates.

  • Career Switchers: Professionals looking to move from traditional industries into the tech sector.
  • Fresh Graduates: Students who feel their university degree did not provide enough practical skills for the job market.
  • Upskillers: Marketing or data professionals who want to formalize their knowledge and learn the latest tools.

Company Vision

RevoU aspires to transform the state of higher education in Southeast Asia.

Their long-term goal is to build a more effective, meritocratic alternative to traditional universities—one that serves the actual needs of the digital economy. Ultimately, they aim to positively impact and change the lives of millions of people by providing access to life-changing career opportunities.

Company Mission

RevoU’s mission is to bridge the skills gap between traditional education and the modern technology industry.

They achieve this by:

  • Equipping Students: Providing ambitious individuals with high-demand, practical digital skills that companies are actively hiring for.
  • Unlocking Potential: Obsessing over student success to help every individual achieve their maximum professional potential, regardless of their background.
  • Empowering Companies: Helping organizations grow by matching them with high-quality, job-ready talent.
